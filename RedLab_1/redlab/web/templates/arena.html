Red vs Blue Arena

Each round: an attacker mutates its controllable parameters — amount positioning, campaign tempo, entity reuse, victim-device share — searching for settings that evade the current detector at a fixed 0.5% false-positive budget. The detector then retrains on whatever got through. This is the closed loop.
