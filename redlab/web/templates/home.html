The three pillars

Identify — Attack Atlas →

A morphological taxonomy across rail × lifecycle stage × GenAI uplift × victim surface, covering India-specific rails, agentic commerce, and attacks on the defence itself.

Generate — Fidelity Report →

An agent-based payment world calibrated on IBM's TabFormer reference corpus, with attack injection matched to the behavioural signature of 28,619 real-labelled reference frauds.

Defend — Defense Console → · Red vs Blue Arena →

30 causal features, a LightGBM detector, and a closed adversarial loop where the attacker adapts against the deployed model and the detector retrains on what got through.
